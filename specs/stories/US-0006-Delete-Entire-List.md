## As a user, I want to delete an entire list and its tasks so that I can remove completed or unneeded projects.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given a list with tasks exists, When the user deletes the list, Then the list and all its associated tasks are removed.

## Notes
Allows removal of whole lists and cleanup of workspace.
