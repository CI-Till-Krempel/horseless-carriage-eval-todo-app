# SPEC-ISSUE-0004: Early PR Engagement and Review Cadence

## Overview
To ensure high quality and adherence to retrospectives, all team members (Architect, QA, DevTeam, ProductOwner) must actively review and comment on sprint backlog PRs early and consistently.

## Guidelines
1. Review sprint backlog PRs promptly upon creation.
2. Provide constructive feedback or explicit sign-offs.
3. Maintain review cadence before feature development begins.
