# User Guide: To-Do App

## Getting Started
Welcome to the To-Do List Web Application. Here you can manage your to-do lists and tasks efficiently.

## Creating Lists
- Enter a name in the "Create List" section and click **Create List**.

## Managing Tasks
- **Add Task:** Enter a task description under any list and click **Add Task**.
- **Complete/Incomplete:** Click **Mark Complete** or **Mark Incomplete** to toggle task status. Completed tasks are visually distinguished with a strikethrough.
- **Delete Task:** Click **Delete** next to any task to remove it.
- **Delete Entire List:** Click **Delete Entire List** to remove the list and all its tasks.
