# Communication and Workflow Adherence Protocols

This document outlines the operational protocols for maintaining clear communication and adhering to story workflow stages across all roles.

## Principles
1. **Strict Stage Adherence**: Stories must transition sequentially through Draft -> Ready -> Implemented -> Reviewed -> Tested -> Accepted without skipping.
2. **Role Ownership**: Each stage is owned by a specific role. Only the designated role may complete their respective stage transition.
3. **Transparent Communication**: All role feedback, reviews, and decisions must be logged and communicated via PR comments and reviews.
